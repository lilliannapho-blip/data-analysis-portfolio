# Coca-Cola-Digital-Marketing-Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes Coca-Cola's digital marketing performance to determine which marketing channels have the greatest impact on sales and provide recommendations for improving marketing allocation.

The dataset contains marketing expenditures across different media channels along with the resulting sales performance. Statistical and financial analysis was used to evaluate the effectiveness and return on investment (ROI) of each channel.

## Objectives 

- Determine which digital marketing channels have the greatest impact on sales
- Compare the effectiveness of mega vs. micro social media influencers
- Identify statistically significant factors associated with sales
- Evaluate the ROI of different marketing channels
- Provide data-driven recommendations for marketing strategy
- Increase sales by 10% by the next fical year

## Analytical Methods

### A/B Testing
Conducted an A/B test comparing two types of social media influencers:
- Mega influencers
- Micro influencers

The analysis evaluates whether there is a statistically significant difference in sales performance between the two groups.

### Regression Analysis
Used regression analysis to:
- Examine the relationship between marketing spending and sales
- Identify statistically significant marketing channels
- Estimate the impact of marketing expenditures on sales

### ROI Analysis
Calculated and compared the return on investment (ROI) of different marketing channels using the regression results.

### Budget Allocation Analysis
Used regrssion results to calcualte the required makreting spend to reach the sales goal

## Key Findings

- There is no significant difference in sales based on influencer type
- TV had the greatest impact on sales and produced the highest ROI
- TV was also the only statistically significant parameter in the regression model
- To achieve an sales increase of 10%, the company would need to increase marketing spend by ~$5,400 for TV advertisment

## Recommendations

Focus allocating our marketing budget more towards TV advertising and consider lowering the marketing spends on radio and social media platforms. 

## Limitations

The results of this analysis may become less applicable over time as digital marketing trends, technologies, and social media platforms continue to evolve.

Changes in consumer behavior, platform popularity, and advertising strategies may affect the relationship between marketing expenditures and sales.
